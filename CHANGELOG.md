### English

- Add the Tencent Docs toolbox to the DSH sidebar and settings, with 43 operations and 5 management tools.
- Open Chrome when sign-in is required, validate and save the session, then close the window. Reuse an existing standalone toolbox sign-in by default.
- Share files and job records between the page and Agent, with live points, transcription time, explicit unlimited entitlements, and file limits.
- Serve the embedded toolbox through the DSH owner connection and manage login windows, the private backend, and registrations through the plugin lifecycle.
